Scope: content, positioning and SEO only. The design, layout, colours, components, checkout code, URLs and existing prices were not changed. Pages were audited from the repository source, not in a browser.

## What the audit found
The repository's 12 products did not match the catalogue you supplied. Resolution, with nothing deleted:
- **7 products renamed and repositioned, slugs kept:** AI Movie Creation Toolkit, Study Abroad Application Toolkit, Get Hired Remotely, Make Your AI Do Anything, AI Automation for Founders & CEOs, Mint Money With Digital Products, Digital Marketing Starter Blueprint. Their URLs no longer describe them exactly (for example /products/ai-productivity-toolkit is now the prompt collection). Kept because you asked to preserve slugs.
- **2 products kept, copy updated:** Faceless YouTube Channel Launch System and the Fully Funded Scholarship toolkit.
- **3 products added with new slugs:** Data Annotation Career Starter Kit, Cybersecurity Career Starter Manual, The Book Launch Machine.
- **3 products not in your catalogue were archived, not deleted:** Budget International Travel Planner, Canva Brand Identity Starter Kit, Personal Productivity & Focus System. Their pages now return not-found and they are out of the menu, sitemap and cards. Their data is still in the code if you want them back.

## Things I could not verify, so the pages say 'planned' or leave them out
- **Contents of 10 of 12 products.** No product files exist. Those pages show topics taken from your own positioning notes, never file lists, and hide item counts. The Faceless YouTube list matches your bundle artwork; the Scholarship list is the one from your original PRD.
- **Make Your AI Do Anything: '1,000 prompts'.** Shown only as the planned size, with an FAQ saying the final count will be confirmed.
- **AI Movie Creation Toolkit: '4K' and 'like a professional'.** Removed from the copy. Add them back only once the workflows are verified to produce that resolution, separating generated resolution from upscaling.
- **Mint Money With Digital Products.** The title reads like an income promise. The page carries a no-income-guarantee disclaimer, but payment providers can object to titles like this. Consider a title without 'money'.
- **Fully Funded Scholarship.** 'Fully Funded' in the title can be read as a promise. The page carries a disclaimer under the hero.
- **Prices.** Existing prices were kept. The 3 new products show 'Price to be announced'.
- **Tools and software requirements, file formats, update policy, support channel, refund terms.** None are confirmed, so none are stated. The product details box appears automatically once you supply them.
- **'Is it suitable outside Nigeria?'** Not answered, because it depends on the checkout provider you choose.

## What was improved on every page
Outcome-led headline; your subtitle as the intro; a problem section with three recognisable situations; the solution as written in your positioning; three modules and three usage steps; who it is for and not for; two product-specific FAQs; a disclaimer under the hero where results could be misread; a unique SEO title and description; breadcrumb structured data on every page; Product structured data only once a product can really be bought; a link to the category's other products.

## Not changed
Design, layout, component structure, global styling, navigation, footer, checkout, prices, existing slugs.
