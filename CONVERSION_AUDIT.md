# DIY Arena conversion audit (all 12 published product pages)

Method: every page's copy and structure was read from the repository source and analysed for repeated wording, thin sections and unanswered objections. Pages were not rendered in a browser. Ratings are judgement calls.
Rules kept: no invented reviews, sales figures, contents or guarantees. Every product is "coming soon", so contents are described as planned.

## Findings that affect every page
| Dimension | Before | After this pass | Still open |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headline | Good, 5 generic | Good, 5 sharpened | none |
| Value proposition | Adequate | Good (hero benefits plus item count) | none |
| Sales narrative | Adequate. Problem was one sentence | Good. Three recognisable situations per page | none |
| Visual presentation | Weak. Plain gradient with a title | Fair. Designed cover | Real bundle mockups needed |
| Product demonstration | Absent | Absent | Sample pages and screenshots need to exist |
| Offer section | Fair. It repeated the before/after text | Good. Lists planned contents | none |
| Objection handling | Fair. 3 answers deferred to "before launch" | Good. Replaced with real answers | File formats, software, updates, support, refund terms |
| Trust signals | Weak. Same text on all pages | Fair. Personalised, honest, no reviews | Real reviews can only come after real sales |
| Pricing | Unvalidated PRD hypotheses | Unchanged | Test with real buyers |
| CTA | Blocked. Button is disabled everywhere | Blocked | Waitlist or checkout needed |

Repeated language found and removed: "templates can be reused" (4 products), "a workbook for planning" (3), "will be listed on this page before launch" (3 FAQ answers), "Anyone expecting guaranteed..." (5 of 12 not-for lines). Hero benefits were also printed twice on the same page, which is fixed.

## The biggest conversion problem (not fixable in code alone)
Every purchase button is disabled, so no page can convert. Visitors who arrive from social posts have no way to stay in touch. Recommended next step: a waitlist form per product (needs the D1 database or an email service) so traffic is captured until each product is ready.

## Price-value flags (hypotheses only, nothing was changed)
- Marketing Toolkit: 6 items at $39, the same price as the 10-item YouTube system.
- Product Creation Blueprint and AI Video Toolkit: 7 items at $39.
- Automation Kit: $49 is the premium price. The PRD requires its workflows to be tested before release, and the page should say so once they are.

## Individual reports
Each lists the verdict, what was changed, and what only the owner can supply.

1. **Faceless YouTube Channel Launch System.** Strongest headline and clearest audience (faceless, beginner). Fixed: three situations, disclaimer about views and income, weak "how long" FAQ replaced with a beginner question, not-for line now says no coaching or editing software. Needs: file format, sample pages.
2. **Fully Funded Scholarship Toolkit.** Strong narrative. Risk: "Fully Funded" in the title can read as a promise. Fixed: disclaimer directly under the hero, situations, shared "guaranteed" wording replaced by a real qualifier. Owner decision: consider renaming the product title. Needs: sample tracker page.
3. **Remote Job Search Toolkit.** Clear and credible. FAQ about job listings duplicated the not-for line, so it was replaced by a portfolio question. Disclaimer about interviews and employment added. Needs: sample CV template.
4. **AI Productivity Toolkit.** Weakest headline ("Put AI to work") and a vague audience. Headline rewritten around planning, research and writing. Evasive tool FAQ replaced. Not-for now says it is prompts and guides, not an app. Needs: which AI tools the examples use.
5. **Digital Product Creation & Launch Blueprint.** Good outcome. Income disclaimer added. Reused-worksheets claim rewritten. Needs: sample worksheet. Price flag above.
6. **Budget International Travel Planner.** Clearest headline of the set. Situations added. Visa-advice not-for kept. Needs: spreadsheet preview and format.
7. **Canva Brand Identity Starter Kit.** Headline now names Canva templates, since the dependency is an objection. Not-for says it is for Canva users. Needs: confirmation that all included assets can legally be distributed, plus template previews.
8. **AI Video Content Creation Toolkit.** Was too close to the YouTube product. Headline now centres on giving AI video tools a plan. Tool FAQ replaced. Disclaimer added. Needs: tools used in the workflow.
9. **Small Business Digital Marketing Toolkit.** Solid but generic. Situations are specific. Leads and sales disclaimer added. Price flag above. Needs: sample calendar.
10. **Personal Productivity & Focus System.** Lowest-stakes page. The unanswered "digital or printable" FAQ was replaced by "is this a course", which it can answer. Needs: format and preview.
11. **Study Abroad Preparation Planner.** Headline had a cliché and is rewritten. Educational-guidance disclaimer added next to the hero. Needs: a sample comparison worksheet.
12. **AI Business Automation Starter Kit.** Highest price, most technical audience. Lead now names n8n and the FAQ says n8n is required. Setup-and-testing disclaimer added. Needs: workflows actually built and tested before it is described as ready.

## Facts only the owner can supply
File formats and software, page counts, update policy, support channel, refund policy, real samples and previews for each product, price validation, and a decision on whether the page can state anything beyond planned contents.
