# Visual assets the redesign still needs

The hero glass is drawn in code (layered translucent panels, a glass disc, a slow sheen). It looks like glass but is not photographic. For the photographic look in the brief, a dedicated image is needed.

## 1. Photographic glassscape backdrop (hero)
Add as `public/brand/hero-glass.webp`, then set `heroBackdrop: "/brand/hero-glass.webp"` in `src/lib/site.ts`. The drawn glass layers switch off automatically.
- Square, 1600 x 1600, under 300 KB as WebP, light overall (white to pale blue-grey) so dark text and the character stay readable.
- No people, text, logos or objects in the centre: the character and light streams sit on top.
- Prompt to give an image generator: "Premium technology studio photograph of a large curved transparent glass sculpture, layered frosted and clear glass panels, soft refraction and reflections, pale blue and white light, subtle violet tint, shallow depth, clean light background, centred empty space in the middle, no text, no people, ultra-sharp, 4K."

## 2. Official brand logos (optional)
The network uses typeset labels and generic icons. If you want real platform marks (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, AI tools), download the official SVG from each company's brand-resources page and follow its usage rules. I did not draw or approximate any logo.

## 3. Real product artwork and previews
Product covers are designed tiles. Real bundle mockups and 3+ preview images per product are needed for the launch scorecard.
